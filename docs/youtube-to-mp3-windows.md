# How do I save YouTube audio as MP3 on Windows?

Paste a supported public YouTube video into Vydrop and choose **Audio → MP3**. The app converts the available source audio locally; no account, API key or subscription is needed.

1. Wait for the video details, then choose **Audio** and MP3.
2. Choose **Automatic** or an offered audio quality. Explicit bitrate choices depend on the estimated source and output codec.
3. Select the save folder and choose **Download**.

## Choose quality honestly

An unknown source offers Automatic. Encoding a compressed source at a higher bitrate cannot recover lost detail. WAV and FLAC are available as lossless output containers but do not turn a lossy source into a lossless recording. M4A and Opus are alternative audio outputs.

For several videos, use [playlist selection](download-youtube-playlist-windows.md) or the queue. This workflow also applies to other [supported public sites](supported-sites.md).
