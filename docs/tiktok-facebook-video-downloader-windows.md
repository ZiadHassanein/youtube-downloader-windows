# How do I send TikTok or Facebook videos to Vydrop?

Vydrop can save supported public TikTok and Facebook videos that do not require sign-in. Send the video's own link, not facebook.com or tiktok.com homepages.

1. Open the specific video or reel and start playback.
2. With the companion enabled, click **Download by Vydrop** beside that player. The extension resolves the selected player's nearby public permalink.
3. Review the resolved link, title and output choices in Vydrop, then choose **Download**.

If detection is ambiguous, use the website's **Share → Copy link** action and paste that link. Never send cookies, passwords or account exports to support. Being signed in to view a video does not mean the downloader can access it anonymously.

See the [Chrome, Edge and Firefox setup guide](browser-extension-setup.md) and [supported-site limits](supported-sites.md). Site changes may require an upstream update; watermark removal is not promised.
