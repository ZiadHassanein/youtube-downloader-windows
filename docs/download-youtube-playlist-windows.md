# How do I download a YouTube playlist on Windows?

Paste a public YouTube playlist link into Vydrop, choose **Whole playlist**, and select the entries to download. Choose video or audio output before starting the selected entries in order.

1. Copy the playlist URL. A video URL containing a list can also offer **This video** and **Whole playlist**.
2. Choose **Choose videos** and retain the entries you want.
3. Choose the format, available quality and save folder. For audio choose **MP3**, M4A, Opus, WAV or FLAC.
4. Start the download and review completed or failed entries in the queue. Open the folder from a completed entry.

## Radio mixes and unavailable entries

A YouTube radio mix changes over time; the app inspects a finite snapshot rather than promising the entire changing mix. Private, removed and sign-in-only entries may be unavailable. A rate-limit or exhausted compatibility error may stop later work so it can be reviewed.

Only compatible completed outputs are eligible for resume. Subtitle settings are part of the output profile. See [audio quality](youtube-to-mp3-windows.md) and [caption formats](youtube-subtitles-srt.md).
